# Notice

This repository contains source code, tests, screenshots, and documentation for the **Smart Communal Room Digital Twin** academic prototype.

The original private PLY scan is not distributed. The runtime Gaussian Splat scene is hosted externally as an unlisted SuperSplat scene and is not included as a repository asset.

The room reconstruction is a digital representation of a real physical room. It should not be extracted, republished, or reused independently of this reviewed prototype without the project owner’s permission and a fresh privacy review.

PlayCanvas, SuperSplat, React, Vite, and all other third-party packages, services, and hosted resources remain subject to their respective licences, notices, and terms. Inclusion or use here does not transfer ownership of those components or services.

All environmental and appliance values shown by the prototype are simulated. Viewer Engagement is an interaction-derived navigation proxy. These values are not measurements from a real property-management system, and the recommendations are not autonomous operational decisions.
